Add an authenticator app

Four steps · nothing is saved until the last one works
EnglishFrançais
1

Install

Get an app on your phone
Any of these three works, they are free, and they hold nothing but codes.
Google Authenticator
Microsoft Authenticator
FreeOTP

It works with no network at all — useful the day the internet box burns out.

2

Scan

Point it at this square
Open the app, choose “add an account”, and hold your phone up to the screen.

This square is good for ten minutes and for one phone. Leaving now means starting again.

or

Instead of scanning

Can’t scan it?
Choose “enter a key” in the app and type these letters. Case does not matter.
The key for your app
JBSW Y3DP EHPK 3PXP
Account name: TOTEM ADMIN (n.•••@totem.app)
The app will show six digits, and change them every thirty seconds.
3

Prove it

Type the code it shows
This is what actually turns the app on. Until this works, nothing has changed on your account.
Refused twice? Your phone’s clock is probably off. Turn on automatic date & time.
4

And then, once

Your ten backup codes
They appear the moment the app is turned on, and never again. Each one opens the door a single time, the day the phone is lost, flat, or left at home.
7K4M · 2QW9PT3B · 8XLD M9VC · 4RHTZ2NF · 6BQK 4DHY · 9WMPQX8L · 3TNR B6KJ · 5FZVW3RD · 7NQH N8TC · 2VLBH5MQ · 9XKF

What you will be told, later

after one is used You used a backup code. Nine left.
when one remains One backup code left. Make new ones — a banner will follow you until you do.
when none remain You have used all ten. Use your app, or ask for a code by email.
You are the super admin: nobody above you can let you back in. Print these, and keep the paper somewhere that is not your phone.
