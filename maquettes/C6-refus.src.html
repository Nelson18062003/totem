When the door says no

Four refusals, and the way out of each one. None of them is a dead end.

The digits don't match
That code doesn't open. Two more tries before we slow things down.

Six digits are easy to misread on a cracked screen. 0 and O, 1 and I — we only ever send digits.

It arrived too late
That code has expired
It was sent at 13:47 and stopped working at 13:57. Nothing is wrong with your account — codes are short-lived on purpose.

The SMS network is sometimes slower than ten minutes. If it happens twice, use the paper in your drawer instead.

Too many tries
Wait one minute, then try again
Five wrong codes in a row. We slow the door down — we do not lock it. This is your shop, and nobody at TOTEM will ever shut you out of it.

Mme Fotso has been told that someone tried five times. If it wasn't you, that message is how she finds out.

The link was already used

An invitation works once. If that was not you, tell Mme Fotso now — she can cancel the account it created, in one gesture, and send you a fresh link.

This is why a link is never enough on its own: a code came to your number too, and without it the link opens nothing.
