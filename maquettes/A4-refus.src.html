What the door never says, whatever happens

Whether the address exists
An unknown address gets the same page and the same wait as a known one. Otherwise the door becomes a list of who works here.
Which part was wrong
Never “right address, wrong code”. The refusal is one sentence, always the same, and it never narrows the guess for anyone.
Anything about the money
No balance, no shop name, no PIN — not before signing in, and never on this page. A door is a door.
